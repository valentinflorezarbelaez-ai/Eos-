## 1. Strict Experimental Protocols & Constraints

1. **READ_ONLY Execution (Level 1 First):** EOS receives only the problem statement and domain constraints. Zero code, zero stack assumptions, zero pre-selected frameworks.
2. **Knowledge Plane Transfer Only:** EOS must apply generalized principles (`LSN-001`, `LSN-002`, `SYS-PRN-001`, `ENV-001`, Data Minimization), NOT past project implementations or mechanical recipes.
3. **6-Category Architectural Decision Framing (Level 2 Requirement):**
   - **Fact (Hecho Conocido):** Empirically verified observation.
   - **Transferred Principle (Principio Transferido):** Cross-domain pattern from Knowledge Plane.
   - **Working Hypothesis (Hipótesis de Trabajo):** Proposed architectural choice to be tested.
   - **Assumption (Supuesto):** Unverified baseline condition.
   - **Uncertainty (Incertidumbre):** Known unknown requiring empirical measurement.
   - **Reversal Trigger (Condición de Reversión):** Specific threshold when decision must be reversed.
4. **Novelty Invariant:** The proposed design must explicitly prove why it is NOT simply a combination of Andes + Sonrisa + FlowDesk.
5. **Predictive Transfer Standard:** Knowledge transfer is validated ONLY if a previously synthesized principle correctly predicts behavior in the new domain.
6. **Falsification & Learning Protocol:** Refuted hypotheses or failed predictions are recorded in the Knowledge Plane under the 9-Point Knowledge Schema as negative learning artifacts.
